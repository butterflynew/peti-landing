A different approach to personal finance

A financial ledger that belongs to you.

Peti is built around a simple idea: your personal financial records should remain under your control. You should be able to record, review, understand, back up, and export your own ledger without making a remote account the center of the experience.

Local-first does not mean fragile. Your ledger stays on your device while backup and export give you ways to keep another copy under your own control.
Your financial ledger

Your records stay close to you.

Peti stores confirmed financial records locally in an encrypted database on your device. The ledger does not need to live on a Peti server for you to use it.

Clear product boundaries

Peti is your ledger. Not your bank.

Your bank remains responsible for your bank accounts and banking services. Peti gives you a private place to record, review, and understand your financial information.

What Peti does not require

  • A Peti financial account
  • A remote financial database
  • Bank credentials
  • Your financial ledger living on a Peti server

What you keep

  • Your local ledger
  • Your app protection
  • Your backups
  • Your exported records
