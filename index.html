Why Peti

Finance without handing over your financial life.

Most financial software is built around an account somewhere. Peti takes a different approach: your ledger is created, encrypted, and maintained on your device.

01

No Account Required

Your personal ledger does not depend on a Peti account. Open the app and manage your finances without creating an online identity.

02

Local-First Ledger

Your financial records remain on your device in an encrypted local database. Peti does not require a cloud-hosted financial ledger.

03

Capture Before Commit

Peti can turn transaction information from supported sources into drafts. Review and confirm before anything becomes part of your ledger.

04

No Bank Credentials

Peti is designed without requiring direct bank credentials or bank-server access to maintain your personal financial record.
