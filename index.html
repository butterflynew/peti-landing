The idea

A financial ledger that belongs to you.

Most financial tools are built around accounts, services, dashboards and infrastructure outside your control. Peti takes a different approach: your financial record is something you should be able to keep, understand and manage yourself.

◈

Private by design

Your personal financial record is treated as private information — not a product to build a profile around.

⌁

On-device first

Peti is designed around keeping your ledger close to you instead of making a remote account the center of it.

✓

Confirm before keeping

Captured information can become a draft first, giving you a chance to review it before it enters your ledger.

Your financial record

See your money as a ledger.

Peti turns individual financial events into a clear record you can return to. Instead of trying to predict your life, it helps you understand what actually happened.

Income, expenses, commitments and captured transactions can live together in one private financial history.

Different by design

Peti is your ledger. Not your bank.

Peti isn't trying to replace your bank, card or payment provider. It gives you a private place to understand the financial activity that matters to you.

Record. Review. Understand.

Your bank handles the money. Peti helps you make sense of the record.
