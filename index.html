A different approach

A private ledger you can actually control.

Your financial records shouldn't need to live in someone else's financial database just to be useful. Peti keeps your ledger local and encrypted, while giving you the tools to back it up and export it when you choose.

◈

Your Ledger, On Your Device

Your confirmed financial records are stored locally in an encrypted database on your device. Peti does not require a cloud-hosted ledger to keep your finances available to you.

◆

You Stay in Control

Peti gives you control over your financial data, including backup and export. Your records aren't locked inside an online account that only Peti can access.

+

Review Before Recording

Financial information can arrive as a draft before it becomes part of your ledger. Review the details, make corrections, and confirm when you're ready.

◇

Private by Design

Peti is designed without requiring bank credentials or a remote financial account to maintain your personal ledger.
